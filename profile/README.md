## What is Manta Client?

Manta Client is a free Minecraft PvP client for Windows. It comes in two parts: a lightweight desktop **launcher** (sign-in, profiles, mods, packs, skins, stats) and a Fabric **client mod** that adds toggleable PvP mods, a movable HUD, practice drills and the Manta menu in game.

It supports **Minecraft 1.21.11** and **26.3** on Fabric.

What makes it different:

- **Ban-safe by design.** No automation, no hidden information, and nothing that changes how other players see you (positions, hitboxes, attack timing). Every feature can be turned off, and off means exactly vanilla.
- **Practice built in.** Drills, bots and 1v1 duels live inside the client.
- **Parties follow you.** Your friends land on whatever server the party leader joins.
- **Capes you earn.** Cosmetics are unlocked by playing, not just bought.

## Launcher

- **Fast setup** – a performance pack (Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ImmediatelyFast, EntityCulling and more) installs automatically, running on the newest Java for extra FPS.
- **Profiles** – one per version or playstyle, each with its own mods, packs, RAM and Java settings.
- **Modrinth built in** – browse mods, resource packs and shaders and add them in one click. Only versions that work with your profile are shown.
- **Texture packs** – browser with previews, side-by-side A/B compare, an organiser and a built-in texture editor.
- **Skins** – 3D skin viewer, skin changer and a paint-on-the-model skin editor.
- **Stats** – PvP stats and tiers pulled from MCPVP and PvPTiers, plus a shareable player card.
- **Instant clips** – press F8 to save the last 15, 30 or 60 seconds, recorded on your graphics card.
- **Network check** – live ping, jitter and packet loss with a plain-English verdict on where lag comes from.
- **FPS benchmark** – tests your settings against a tuned alternative and tells you which is faster.

## In game

- **Manta menu** (Right Shift) – every mod in one searchable menu, each with its own settings, keybind and performance label.
- **HUD** – keystrokes, CPS, FPS, ping, coordinates, armour, potions, combo counter, now playing and more. Drag anything around, including Minecraft's own hotbar, chat and scoreboard. Separate PvP and SMP layouts.
- **Visuals** – custom crosshair, hit colour, hitbox and block outline colours, low fire, fullbright, no fog, zoom, totem particle controls.
- **Practice and duels** – practice worlds with drills for combos, strafing, aim, spear lunges and more, plus a private 1v1 duels world.
- **Waypoints** – press B to mark a spot; death points are saved automatically.
- **Chat** – timestamps, compact chat, name highlights, clickable coordinates, and warnings on scam links.
- **Toolbox** – brewing and enchanting guides and a Minecraft wiki, in game and in the launcher.
- **Discord status** – shows you're playing Minecraft with Manta Client.

## Is it safe?

- You sign in on **Microsoft's own page**. Manta never sees or stores your password.
- Every mod in your profile is **scanned for known malware** (including fractureiser) before launch.
- Updates are **signed and checked** before they install.
- Manta never changes how you appear to other players or the server, which is what anticheats check. Servers running the Manta plugin can switch individual features off.
